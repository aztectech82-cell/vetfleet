ABOUT

Technical Knowledge, Clearly Taught and Applied to Planning

Veteran Fleet Technologies, LLC is a veteran-owned advisory and training firm led by Marcos Alvarez Guzman. Our focus is electric and hybrid vehicle diagnostics education, CARB compliance consulting, curriculum development, and heavy-duty fleet maintenance program consulting.

Marcos brings independently earned CARB and ASE credentials, U.S. Army service, and an MBA to technical education and advisory engagements.

Based in Riverside, we welcome inquiries from educators, fleet managers, and government purchasing teams across Southern California. Remote consulting and training are available nationwide.
